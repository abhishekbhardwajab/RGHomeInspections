A full assessment, matched to the property type.

Every inspection follows the same disciplined process — we simply scope it to the building in front of us, whether that's a single detached home or a multi-unit rental.

The Process

Four steps, start to finish.

No jargon, no rush. You'll understand exactly what was found and what it means for the property.

01 / BOOK

Schedule a time

Call or send your details online. We confirm a time that works around your closing date or listing deadline.

02 / INSPECT

On-site assessment

A hands-on inspection of the roof, structure, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, and safety systems — inside and out.

03 / REPORT

Photo-documented report

A clear, organized report with photos of every finding, delivered the same week — usually within 24 hours.

04 / WALKTHROUGH

Plain-language walkthrough

We go through the findings together by phone or in person, so nothing in the report is a mystery.
